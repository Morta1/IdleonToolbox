Patch notes

More patch notes

Features

  • The whole site is rebuilt, every page: new cards, tables and dialogs, and it all fits a phone
  • The first visit after this update opens a short story of what changed, with your own characters, and a tour of the new layout.
  • The left menu is gone: Worlds, Account and Tools in the top bar open every page
  • A theme switch: Dark, Light or System, in the user menu, Settings and the footer
  • Dashboard: timers are one table from Ready now to Later, and tapping an alert or timer shows its meaning and settings
  • Characters opens on your first character, and two or more line up section by section
  • Settings that change a page's numbers moved behind one button beside the page title
  • Long lists have a toolbar to search, filter, sort and switch views
  • Numbers follow one rule: below 1M they show their decimals, from 1M up the game's short form
  • Sign in and Search are rebuilt: pick how you sign in and follow short steps; Search shows pinned and recent pages, and finds the closest match for a typo

Fixes

  • Part of your save failing to load now shows that page at zero with one note, and numbers that rely on it show - instead of a wrong number
  • Signed-out pages no longer show NaN, undefined or repeated weeks
  • Game text and numbers now read as in the game on many pages, including Gaming, Sailing, Compass, Tesseract and Minehead
  • Dashboard timers no longer count the save's age twice, and a goal just short of its target no longer reads as reached

Features

  • Cape Vine: new World 4 page with your capes, Golden Vines and bonuses, and its bonuses now count in your stats
  • Rift: Research Skill Mastery
  • Dashboard: alerts for capes missing from the Cape Vine and a Golden Vine ready to unlock

Fixes

  • Printer: Yet Another Printer Multi now counts
  • Masterclass drops: Killroy now gives its full bonus
  • Spelunking: the Pit O Pages talent now counts for every character
  • Farming: Land Rank EXP now includes the Exotic Market, Menagerie and talent bonuses
  • Damage: Eclectic Sigils now add their Research Grid damage bonus
  • Royal Guardian: Expanded Barracks gives unit slots up to Lv 6
  • Hole: the Temple shows large torch counts and costs in M and B
  • Dashboard: Daily and Weekly reset timers no longer run late on older saves

Fixes

  • Guild History: a guild page keeps its own title instead of switching back to the generic one
  • Login: email and Apple sign-ins are tracked correctly, and closing the login dialog cancels a Google sign-in in progress
  • Login: the numpad Enter key submits the email form, and password managers can fill it
  • Pinned pages: unpinning a page no longer removes other pins
  • Hole, Royal Armory and Highscores: descriptions no longer show raw @, $ or } symbols
  • Worship: the Spelunking POW and Research EXP totalizer stats show icons
  • Summoning: the Total bonus of Red Essence Ragin', Teal Tirade, Teal Knowledge, Sharpened Spikes and Cost Laundering now matches the game
  • Summoning: army damage now counts Unit Obliteration
