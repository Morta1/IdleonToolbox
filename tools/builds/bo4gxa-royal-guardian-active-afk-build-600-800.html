Royal Guardian — Active Orb Build

Use:

Guardian Disciple

↓

Regal Intervention

↓

Orb of Verisimilitude

↓

Lil' Orblets

Guardian Disciple and Regal Intervention improve your active clear and Orb performance.

Orb of Verisimilitude should be raised until the additional Orb duration actually lets you reach another useful kill breakpoint.

Example:

2,850 Orb Kills → 3,000

is much more useful than:

3,050 → 3,100

if both still produce the same number of Orblet thresholds.

Royal Guardian — Outpost Push

When progressing Kingdom Outposts:

Warbound Politics — Highest Priority

Move Royal Guardian points away from other activities if necessary.

Once the Outpost is finished, those points can be respecced.
